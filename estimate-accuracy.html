Estimate vs Actual for Jira

Compares the original estimate with the time logged on resolved issues, and checks whether story points predict how long work really takes.

Set up

Add the gadget, open its three-dot menu, choose Configure and fill in:

  • Which issues? (JQL). Which issues count. The default is statusCategory = Done. Use it to limit the gadget to a project, a team or an issue type.
  • Period. How far back to look at resolved issues: the last 30, 60, 90, 180 or 365 days (default 90).

What the numbers mean

FigureDefinition
Within ±25%Share of issues where time spent was between 75% and 125% of the original estimate
Typical overrunMedian of time spent divided by original estimate
Total biasTotal time spent against total estimated, as a percentage. Positive means more time was spent than estimated
Overrun by estimate sizeMedian overrun for each estimate size: up to 2 h, 2 to 4 h, 4 to 8 h, 1 to 3 days, over 3 days. Bars above 1.25 are orange
By assigneeIssues, share within ±25% and bias for each person with at least three issues (up to 12 people)
Biggest overrunsIssues with an estimate of an hour or more, ordered by time spent divided by estimate. Only overruns are listed
Cycle time by story pointsFor each story point size, the range from the 25th to the 75th percentile and the median, from the first move into an In Progress status to resolution. Values are rounded up to the next of 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 or 21, and larger ones are grouped as 21+, so a 4 appears with the 5s
Points vs effortRank correlation (Spearman) between story points and cycle time, with a short reading

Only issues with both an original estimate and logged time are used for the time figures. Issues that went from To Do straight to Done have no start for cycle time and are left out of it: counting from creation would measure waiting in the backlog, not work.

Work that was moved to Done in a workflow that never sets Resolution is still found. Its resolution date is the moment it entered the Done category.

Limits

  • Up to 500 issues resolved inside the period are read. If there are more, the gadget says so and states the dates of the issues it read instead of the period.
  • The rank correlation needs at least five issues with points and some variation. Below that it shows a dash.
  • The by-assignee table is there to find estimates worth discussing. It is a poor basis for judging people, because the issues each person gets are rarely comparable.
