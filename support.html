Before you write

Most questions are answered on the gadget's page: what each number means, the limits (issue caps and the time limit) and what the gadget shows when it hits one.

  • Scope Creep & Churn
  • Bug Aging & Reopen Rate
  • Estimate vs Actual

What to include in a report

  • The gadget name and the version shown in your Jira app list.
  • What you expected and what you saw. A screenshot of the gadget helps; check it for data you do not want to share first.
  • For the bug and estimate gadgets, the JQL filter in the settings.
  • For Scope Creep & Churn, the board type (company-managed or team-managed) and whether the sprint is active or closed.

Please do not send issue contents or credentials. Nothing in the gadgets needs them.

Known behaviour that is not a bug

  • A note above the numbers saying the gadget "answered slowly" or "more than N" means a limit was reached. The gadget shows what it read. Narrow the JQL filter or pick a smaller period.
  • A message saying the app "has used up its hourly allowance of Jira calls, which every site that uses it shares" means Jira's per-hour limit for the whole app was reached. A message saying "This site has used up the app's hourly allowance" means the limit for your site was reached. Data can show again when the next hour starts (UTC); nothing needs to be changed.
  • Dashboards show different numbers to different people when their Jira permissions differ.
  • Day and month buckets use UTC.
